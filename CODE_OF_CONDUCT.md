# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect different viewpoints, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Protect other people's privacy and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained hostile communication.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Self-promotion or spam.
- Offering or requesting financial or other compensation in exchange for code contributions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner, [@TheCodeFighter](https://github.com/TheCodeFighter).

Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where possible. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
